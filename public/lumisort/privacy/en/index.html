Lumi Sort · Privacy Policy

Expired records are eligible for periodic cleanup. Specific records subject to legal preservation are handled separately. Account deletion or the end of a period does not mean every copy is erased simultaneously or immediately.

Lumi Sort is a water-sort puzzle provided by MASHONG (representative and privacy contact: Ma Suyong). Contact: mso0504@naver.com. Applies to Lumi Sort (com.mashong.lumisort). Effective: October 25, 2026. Updated: October 27, 2026.

Information and purposes

We use account and installation identifiers, Apple sign-in links when used, game progress, virtual-currency ledgers, and purchase, refund and ad-reward records to operate accounts, synchronize progress, process grants and refunds, and prevent abuse. Support correspondence includes the email address and content you send. Do not send payment card numbers to the app server.

Advertising and tracking choices

Google Mobile Ads may process device identifiers, ad and app interactions, approximate location inferred from IP addresses, and crash and performance diagnostics for advertising and analytics. After any required regional consent flow (UMP), iOS tracking permission is requested if your choice is not yet determined. If allowed, personalized ads may use IDFA. If denied, the app is configured to disable personalization and the publisher first-party identifier. You can still use the game and rewarded-ad features without allowing tracking, although ad availability depends on inventory and consent status. Change this app's choice in iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Starting with version 1.0.2, non-rewarded interstitial ads may appear during gameplay in addition to optional rewarded ads. Ticket purchases grant seven days without these interstitials on that device. Advertising data processing and privacy choices follow the Google advertising description above.

Account deletion

Request deletion in Settings > Account > Delete Account. After the server confirms deletion, the app clears login links and authentication sessions, unlinks the installation identifier from the account, and forfeits remaining tickets and undos. The current attempt is detached. Completed stages, best stars, preferences such as language and sound, and the board layout may remain on the device. We do not guarantee recovery of the deleted account’s server records or merging them into a new account. Apple-linked accounts require Apple reconfirmation. Some pseudonymous identifiers and purchase or reward records remain under the retention rules below; deletion does not erase every record immediately.

Retention and disposal

Expired or revoked authentication sessions, nonces and account-switch tickets are eligible for cleanup after 7 days. Deleted-account game progress is eligible after 30 days from the deletion request. Raw server-side verification (SSV) callback text is cleared after 30 days from receipt; ad-session and SSV callback rows are eligible after 180 days from creation or receipt. The implementation deletes non-payment currency ledger entries, device and grant records, wallets, daily allocations, deletion-processing records and remaining related records in periodic cleanup after 180 days from the account deletion request. Deleted accounts with no paid records are also removed. Where paid records remain, a pseudonymous account identifier is retained to link the transactions. Pseudonymization does not make the records fully anonymous. Minimum evidence of paid contracts, withdrawals, payments, ticket supply and refunds follows a five-year rule under Article 6 of Korea’s e-commerce enforcement decree. Purchase records are eligible for cleanup five calendar years after the latest grant, completion or refund processing for that purchase. Paid-currency ledger entries and transaction-linked store notifications use their individual creation or receipt date. Store notifications unrelated to a transaction are eligible after 180 days. Login or account deletion does not restart every record’s clock; free-currency, ad records and notification text are not all extended to five years. Consumer complaint/dispute records are managed for three years after closure. For an actual proceeding or investigation requiring further preservation, specific records, reasons and end conditions are recorded and records disposed of when the purpose ends. Expired electronic records are irrecoverably deleted and paper shredded. Statutorily retained records of deleted accounts are separated from service-use records with restricted access.

Providers, international processing and backups

The selected Neon database is in AWS Singapore; its verified point-in-time restore history is six hours. The project currently has zero additional snapshots, no snapshot schedule and no separate periodic dump job in its deployment configuration. The operating rule is not to create an additional long-term backup. This does not mean every provider replica, security log or unverified manual copy disappears after six hours. If recovery occurs, previously received deletion requests must be reapplied.
