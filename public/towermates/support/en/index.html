Accounts and progress

A guest account uses a secret stored in your device keychain. Reinstalling may retain access if that secret remains, but this is not guaranteed. Link Apple or Google in Settings to recover your account on another device. A guest account cannot be recovered after signing out.

Delete Account

Open Settings (gear, top right), tap Delete Account, read the notice and tap again to continue. An Apple-linked account requires Apple sign-in confirmation. Your server account and game progress are deleted and cannot be restored. See the privacy policy for information that may remain.

Language and connection

Settings → Language switches between Korean, English and Japanese. A language change during a match takes effect on the next launch. Internet access is required for solo and cooperative play. The battle server is in Seoul, South Korea; players elsewhere may experience higher latency. If a game data mismatch message appears, update the app in the App Store or TestFlight.

Ads and tracking

Non-personalized ads may appear even if you deny tracking. The app requests non-personalized ads (NPA=1), disables ad personalization and publisher first-party ID, and requests ads without the advertising identifier when tracking is denied. You can still play and buy or restore purchases. Change your tracking choice in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Starting with version 1.0.3, all ad requests also include the restricted data processing signal RDP=1.

Remove Ads and Restore Purchase

Remove Ads is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. Check the price shown by the App Store before buying. To restore, sign into the App Store with the Apple Account used to purchase and choose Restore Purchase in the app Settings. This does not restore game progress. Request refunds through Apple; the operator cannot guarantee approval or restoration.

Sound

Adjust music and effects separately in Settings. If silent, check your device volume and silent mode.
