TowerMates Privacy Policy

Operator: MASHONG · Effective date: October 29, 2026.

TowerMates is a cooperative tower defense game for 1–4 players. Cloudflare and AWS support accounts, room lists and game progress. Google processes information for ads, and Apple processes purchases.

Information and purposes

  • Random device secret: keeps guest access. Stored in the device keychain; only a hash is stored on the server.
  • Nickname (up to 24 characters): shown in rooms, lobbies and matches; stored on the account server and used by the battle server.
  • Apple or Google provider identifier, if linked: optional account recovery across devices. The account server does not store your email or real name.
  • Session token hashes, expiry, account creation and last sign-in times: account access and operation.
  • Game progress: hero tokens and transactions, ability levels, owned/equipped skills, heroes and colors, stage clears, match results and rewards.
  • Room creation records (account, room name and status): room limits and cleanup.
  • Connection IP addresses: rate limits to prevent abuse of sign-in, creation and deletion. Cleanup starts after approximately one day; actual retention can be longer depending on cleanup timing.
  • Battle connection logs (nickname and slot): match operation and troubleshooting. Old logs are overwritten when capacity limits are reached.
  • Volume, text size, ad-removal state and reconnect tokens: stored on the device.

The operator’s game features do not collect email, real names, phone numbers, precise location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera information. No separate analytics or crash-reporting SDK is used; the Google advertising SDK may process the information described below. Information is not sold or provided beyond what is needed for ads, purchases and server operation.

Advertising and tracking choices

Google AdMob interstitial ads are shown once when starting a match. Devices with Remove Ads do not show ads. Denying iOS App Tracking Transparency (ATT) does not remove ads: you can still play and buy or restore purchases.

All ad requests carry the non-personalized signal NPA=1. Ad personalization and Google publisher first-party ID are disabled, and the maximum ad content rating is G. When tracking is denied, requests do not use the advertising identifier (IDFA). If allowed, IDFA may be used for ad delivery and measurement. Processing needed to deliver and measure ads can still occur. Change permission in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Withdrawal does not immediately erase information already sent.

Starting with version 1.0.3, all ad requests also include the restricted data processing signal RDP=1. This is separate from the non-personalized advertising signal.

The Google advertising SDK may process approximate location inferred from IP addresses, device identifiers, ad impressions and interactions, app usage and performance, and crash or diagnostic information. Device identifiers, approximate location and advertising or interaction data may be linked to device identifiers. Such processing can occur even without a separate analytics SDK. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Purchases

Remove Ads is a one-time purchase processed by Apple. The operator receives no card details and stores no purchase receipts on its server. Ownership is checked through Apple purchase history and restored through Restore Purchase. Purchases are not linked to the game account.

Retention and deletion

Account information and progress remain until you delete the account in Settings → Delete Account. Deletion removes account/sign-in information, nickname, progress, currency and room creation records from the account server, and removes the device sign-in key. Apple-linked accounts require another Apple sign-in confirmation and Apple token revocation.

Some information may remain: local preferences until the app is deleted; Apple purchase history; information processed by Apple or Google advertising SDKs; rotating server logs; and IP records awaiting cleanup. Match records involving other players may remain only as statistics separated from the account.

Service providers

  • Cloudflare: account and room-list services.
  • Amazon Web Services: battle server in Seoul, South Korea.
  • Google: AdMob and Google sign-in.
  • Apple: purchases and Sign in with Apple.

Audience, changes and contact

This is not an app specifically for children. Availability is determined by the App Store listing. Changes in processing will be reflected on this page with an effective date, and in the app when needed.
