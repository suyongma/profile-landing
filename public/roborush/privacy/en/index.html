Robo Rush Privacy Policy

Applies to: Robo Rush (Korean App Store name "광고그거"; iOS com.suyongma.g1tower · Android com.suyongma.roborush) · Effective: November 18, 2026 · Provided by MASHONG

Robo Rush (the "App") is a game you can play without signing up. The App has no game server run by MASHONG (the "Operator"), and your progress is stored only on your device. This policy explains what stays on your device and what external services (Google, Apple and Cloudflare) process. This page replaces the shared notice used until November 18, 2026 (the 광고그거 section of mashong.com/apps/privacy/).

1. Information the Operator collects directly

The App does not ask for your name, email address or phone number, does not access your photos, contacts, microphone, camera or location, and does not send your progress to an Operator server. The Operator only receives what you choose to send in a support email (section 8).

2. Information stored only on your device

  • Game progress: unlocked stages, records per stage, play counts and the hidden character unlock
  • Settings: music and sound effect volume, screen shake, vibration and language
  • Whether you bought Remove Ads, and a counter used to space out interstitial ads
  • How many times, and when last, the App asked you to rate it
  • Downloaded art and music packs (section 3)

This information is deleted when you delete the App. If device backups (such as iCloud or Android Auto Backup) are on, it may be included in those backups, which follow Apple's and Google's settings and policies.

3. Downloading art and music packs (Cloudflare)

From version 1.5.0, art and music for some worlds are downloaded from cdn.mashong.com. At startup the App shows the download size, and downloads only if you choose to. This address is served by Cloudflare (a US company with a global network). The request includes your IP address and the standard connection information your device sends; the App does not send account or game progress information. The Operator does not use these request records to identify you or analyze them separately. Connection records that Cloudflare processes for delivery and security follow Cloudflare's privacy policy.

4. Ads (Google AdMob)

The App shows Google AdMob interstitial ads between stages. Google's ads SDK may process advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location estimated from the IP address, device and OS information, ad impressions and interactions, and diagnostics for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention (iOS disclosure, Android disclosure). This is not the same as reading GPS location through a location permission.

  • iOS: before starting the ads SDK, the App asks for tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency). If you allow it, personalized ads may be shown using your advertising identifier. If you do not, you can still play, and non-personalized ads may be shown. You can change this in iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Android: ad requests are set to non-personalized ads. The ads SDK may process your Android advertising ID for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. You can reset or delete it in your device settings under Google > Ads.
  • After you buy Remove Ads, the App no longer requests ads.

5. Purchases (Remove Ads)

Remove Ads is processed by Apple In-App Purchase on iOS and Google Play Billing on Android. The Operator does not receive your card details or your Apple or Google account password, and purchase records are not sent to an Operator server. The App checks your purchase with the store on your device, and you can get it back with Restore Purchases in Settings. Refunds are requested from Apple or Google Play.

6. Rating prompt

After you have cleared several stages, the system rating prompt (App Store rating or Google Play In-App Review) may appear when you clear a new stage for the first time. Apple and Google decide whether to show it and process any rating; the App cannot tell whether you rated it.

7. Permissions

The Android app uses internet and network state (ads and pack downloads), the advertising ID and Google Play Billing, along with some system permissions required by the ads SDK. The iOS app only asks for tracking permission. The App does not use camera, microphone, photos, contacts or location permissions.

8. Your rights and contact

You can request access, correction, deletion or suspension of processing of your personal information, or withdraw consent, by emailing mso0504@naver.com. The Operator holds no game records, so you can erase them by deleting the App. When contacting us, please include the app name and version, your device and OS version, your question and an email address for our reply. Do not send passwords, verification codes, card numbers or full receipts.

Operator: MASHONG · Representative and privacy contact: Ma Suyong · mso0504@naver.com

9. Children

The App is for a general audience and is not designed specifically for children. Store age ratings do not set a separate minimum age for using the App.

10. Users outside Korea

Robo Rush is available in Korea, Japan and the United States, and we plan to make it available in Canada, Australia, New Zealand, Singapore, the Philippines, Malaysia, India, Hong Kong and South Africa. Availability and timing may differ by store. MASHONG (Korea) decides how the information described in this policy is used. Apple, Google and Cloudflare process information as described above, under their own terms.

Legal bases (under applicable laws): providing the game, downloading art and music packs, Remove Ads purchases and restores, and replying to your support messages are based on performing our contract with you; delivery security, service stability and ad fraud prevention on legitimate interests; and personalized ads on iOS on your tracking permission (consent). We do not make decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you based solely on automated processing.

Your rights: depending on where you live, you can ask to access, correct or delete your information, restrict its use, receive information you provided in a portable form, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. You can withdraw consent at any time; this does not affect earlier processing. Send requests to mso0504@naver.com. We reply within the period your local law requires.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to your local data protection authority, such as the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner, the New Zealand Privacy Commissioner, Singapore's Personal Data Protection Commission, the Philippines' National Privacy Commission, Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Commissioner, the Data Protection Board of India, Hong Kong's Office of the Privacy Commissioner for Personal Data or South Africa's Information Regulator. We would appreciate the chance to address your concern first.

International transfers: the Operator processes only support emails, in Korea. Ad information is processed in countries where Google operates, including the United States; purchase and device backup information in countries where Apple and Google operate; and pack download connection information on Cloudflare's global network. These countries may not provide the same level of protection as yours. Contact us for details.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the new version on this page with a new effective date, and changes in how the App handles information will also be noted in the App's update notes.
